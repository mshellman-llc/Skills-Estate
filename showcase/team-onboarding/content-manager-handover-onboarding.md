## Request

We have a new content marketing manager, Dana Okafor, starting Monday 7 December, and Tomas Reyes, who currently runs all our content, leaves on 18 December. Can you build the onboarding plan so Dana ramps over three months and we capture what Tomas knows before he goes? Role brief and team notes are attached.

## Output

### Onboarding plan: Dana Okafor, content marketing manager

Dana has only 10 working days (7 to 18 Dec) with Tomas, and the company closes 24 Dec to 1 Jan. So the plan front-loads handover: Dana learns by running Tomas's live tasks, and writes down the process as they go. Dana publishes a post in week 1, sends the December newsletter on 17 Dec with Tomas watching, and shows on 16 and 18 Dec that the written steps work without him.

#### Decisions and assumptions for Aisha

- **Tomas's time.** The notes allow about 6 hours a week for handover. That is too little for five undocumented processes in two weeks. I planned for about 12 hours in week 1 and 10 in week 2. If Tomas can't give that, drop the freelancer introductions first, and keep the newsletter and CMS sessions.
- **Seats.** The notes say the CMS editor seat, email platform admin seat and a named SEO seat are needed. I assumed none exist yet and put them on the pre-day-1 list. The shared SEO login is used until the named seat exists.
- **Laptop.** Due 25 Nov. If it is late, Dana needs a loaner by 4 Dec.
- **Review dates.** The holiday closure falls inside month 1, so reviews are set by working time: day 30 is the week of 11 Jan, day 60 the week of 8 Feb, day 90 the week of 8 Mar. These are my dates; adjust them if Aisha prefers calendar days.
- **Not in the notes.** Freelancer rates and contract terms, the content of the SEO keyword sheet, and whether the January newsletter is already drafted. Tomas should confirm each in handover session 4.
- **Buddy.** Luis Ferreira, at about 2 hours a week, is named buddy. Aisha stays responsible for evaluation.

#### 4. Handover plan (the knowledge-transfer layer)

The notes name four things Tomas keeps mostly in his head: freelancer briefs, the SEO keyword sheet, the newsletter send checklist and the CMS publishing steps. Each is transferred by the same method: Tomas does the task, Dana writes the steps, then Dana does the next instance alone while Tomas reads the draft. A document counts as finished only after Dana has used it.

| # | When | Topic | Dana's written output | Proof it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon 7 Dec | CMS publishing | One-page publish checklist: draft, SEO fields, images, preview, publish, redirect check | Dana publishes the refreshed post on Thu 10 Dec |
| 2 | Tue 8 Dec | Newsletter send checklist | Step list from segment to send, including who proofreads and what to test | Dana builds the 17 Dec newsletter from it |
| 3 | Wed 9 Dec | SEO keyword sheet | One page: how keywords are chosen, how often the sheet is updated, what counts as a priority term | Dana proposes keywords for one January post; Tomas confirms |
| 4 | Fri 11 Dec | Freelancer briefs | A brief template and a note on each writer's strengths, turnaround and rate (rates from Tomas) | Dana writes the next brief for Ravi or Elena |
| 5 | Mon 14 Dec | Calendar logic | Why items are scheduled when they are; what is flexible | Dana drafts the January slots |
| 6 | Tue 15 Dec | Open items | List of anything in flight: promised pieces, late invoices, pending replies | Tomas signs the list |
| 7 | Wed 16 Dec | Dry run | None | Dana publishes a post using only the written checklist; Tomas observes |
| 8 | Thu 17 Dec | December newsletter | Updated send checklist | Dana sends it; Tomas watches and does not step in unless there is an error |
| 9 | Fri 18 Dec | Exit review | Final index of the runbook | Aisha, Tomas and Dana check every runbook page against the four topics above |

Introductions to Ravi and Elena happen by video call in the week of 14 Dec, 20 minutes each, led by Tomas. Joanna Pike's case-study meeting happens in week 1 or 2, whichever she has free.

Tomas's handover hours are protected: Aisha moves his remaining content tasks to Dana or pauses them from 7 Dec.

#### 5. Buddy guide (Luis Ferreira)

Luis is not Dana's manager. He is the person Dana can ask without it counting as a performance signal. Time budget: about 2 hours a week for 12 weeks.

- **Day 1:** tools tour and lunch (already scheduled).
- **Weeks 1 and 2:** a 20-minute chat on Wednesday and Friday. Ask what was confusing that week and what Dana hasn't raised with Aisha or Tomas.
- **Product context:** walk Dana through current launches and who to ask about product detail. Dana writes launch copy with him in January.
- **Introductions:** to product, customer success and the web developer (Mei is in on Tuesday and Thursday only).
- **Reports to Aisha:** only things Dana agrees can be shared, or a risk that Dana is stuck. Buddy conversations are otherwise private.
- **Weeks 3 to 12:** one 30-minute chat a week, then every two weeks from week 7.
- **When Luis is overloaded:** he tells Aisha, who reassigns his launch work. A buddy with no time is a known failure in onboarding, so this is the first thing to cut elsewhere.

- Publishing two posts a week, with the runbook updated after each.
- January newsletter sent by Dana.

**By day 60 (week of 8 Feb)**

- First case study published, second in draft.

**By day 90 (week of 8 Mar)**
- Two case studies shipped.
- Aisha has approved Dana's March calendar, and Dana owns it.

- Dana has raised at least one disagreement or change request with Aisha, and Aisha has answered it.
