# Sow quality

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Read the complete document and its controlling quote, approved template, and scope baseline. Preserve legal meaning, parties, amounts, dates, approval state, obligations, and required disclosures. Distinguish an editorial correction from a commercial or legal decision.

Give each mechanic one authoritative home and use accurate cross-references elsewhere. Remove duplication only when it does not remove an obligation or alter how clauses interact. Check that deliverables have observable acceptance, responsibilities have owners, and dependencies are distinguished from promises.

Review the basis of estimate: supported counts and volumes, their source, how uncertainty affects effort, and the process for handling a changed assumption. Distinguish a volume change within an existing workstream from adding a new workstream. Do not insert automatic charges or change-order rights that the approved terms do not grant.

Check amounts, milestone totals, dates, defined terms, attachments, exclusions, and clause references. Return edits plus material unresolved decisions with exact locations. Use human-facing writing review without treating word counts or a phrase scanner as legal or factual validation. An edited SOW remains unapproved until its owner approves it.
