# Structural de-slop

Review structure in addition to word patterns. Writing Integrity handles the general prose review. This module checks repeated mechanics, justification tails and internal commentary while preserving meaning and obligations.

## Repetition-as-thoroughness

Find repeated distinctive phrases across the entire document, including tables and attachments. A repeated term or cross-reference is not a repeated mechanic. Compare each full passage, its controlling source and legal role before changing it. Use the ownership map in `section-ownership.md`; retain intentional repetitions and mandated wording. Report before/after counts alongside the substantive review, not as proof of correctness.

## Clause-justification

A reason that merely repeats a rule's purpose may be removable. A reason that defines a dependency, defends an exception, communicates a risk or carries an agreed qualification must remain. Inspect the actual terms rather than guessing a standard acceptance period or default policy. Proposed changes to an obligation need its authorized owner's decision.

Synthetic example: “Keep the source checksum with the review result so that the result identifies its source.” If that repeats a defined provenance requirement elsewhere, a pointer may be enough. If it establishes the only traceability requirement, cutting it loses meaning.

## Verb piles and internal commentary

Compare adjacent verbs before collapsing them: similar-looking verbs can create different obligations. Remove duplicated explanations only after checking scope and exceptions. Keep required disclosures, agreed pricing explanations and responsibility boundaries even if they discuss internal operations. Internal commentary with no reader-facing or contractual purpose can be proposed for deletion.

Synthetic example: “The sample export omits scratch notes. Scratch notes are not exported.” One statement suffices only if both describe the same output and no exception is lost.

## Verify

Re-run the scanner, review remaining candidates and compare parties, figures, dates, definitions, obligations and references against the original and controlling inputs. Scanner success is a bounded diagnostic result. It is not legal review or approval.
