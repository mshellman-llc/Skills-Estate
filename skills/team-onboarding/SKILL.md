# Team onboarding

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Read the role, responsibilities, start date, team context, expected outcomes, and available onboarding material. Identify constraints such as part-time hours, contract scope, access prerequisites, and key-person knowledge risks.

Plan belonging and access during the first week, business and system context during the first month, supported contribution as readiness permits, and independent ownership during months two and three. Tailor the sequence to the role rather than imposing one timeline on every hire.

For each milestone, name the output, responsible guide, prerequisite, and observable evidence of readiness. Include feedback conversations, a buddy or named contact where available, and a route for unresolved access or knowledge gaps. Do not invent assigned people or grant access.

Deliver a usable schedule and ownership checklist. Distinguish planned training from completed training and demonstrated readiness from attendance. This skill does not make hiring or performance decisions.
