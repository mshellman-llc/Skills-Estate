# Writing style builder

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Confirm the intended person, use, sample set, and permission to analyze it. Connector access alone does not authorize searching a person's messages for a voice profile. Use supplied or explicitly authorized material and keep it within the agreed storage boundary.

Identify repeated choices in structure, vocabulary, cadence, directness, humor, explanation, and formatting. Separate the person's voice from a client's requirements, quoted text, and the conventions of a single format. Record counterexamples and uncertainty rather than turning every observed phrase into a rule.

Create a concise draft profile with evidence-backed patterns, applicable contexts, protected meaning, and examples drawn only from authorized material. Use an insufficient-evidence outcome when the sample cannot support a stable rule. Have the person confirm the profile before treating it as their governing voice.

For updates, preserve the prior version and record what changed and why. Revocation removes the profile from active use through the agreed storage process. Do not put raw personal samples into the shared skills repository. Applying an approved profile belongs to personal-writing-style-runtime.
