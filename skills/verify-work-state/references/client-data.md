# Client records and migrations

Confirm the client and lifecycle context before reading or writing. Preserve
source identity, provenance, dates, contradictions, and unresolved gaps. Never
promote an inference into canonical client truth merely to complete a migration.

Inventory and report mode are read-only. A proposed mapping is not approval to
move, rewrite, rename, or delete client material. Before an authorized migration,
record source counts and hashes, destination mapping, conflicts, rollback
information, and privacy boundaries. Verify destination counts and hashes after
the write.

Keep detailed client paths and inventories out of shared repository history.
Report whether canonical records, mirrors, permissions, and source files changed.
