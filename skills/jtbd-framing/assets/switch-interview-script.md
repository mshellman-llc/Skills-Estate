# Switch interview script

Interview people who switched to or away from the product, or a competitor, within the last 90 days and who used both options. Plan 60 to 90 minutes, record only with consent, and ask about one specific decision. Ask when things happened as well as what happened, and ask for the last specific time rather than the usual habit.

## Opening

"I'd like to walk through your decision to start using [the product or the alternative], step by step. I'll ask a lot about when things happened, because I'm trying to rebuild the timeline. Let's start at the beginning: take me back to the first time you thought you needed something like this. What was happening that day?"

## Timeline

**First thought.** Looks for push from the current situation.
- When did you first realize you needed something different?
- What was happening that day?
- What were you using before?
- What made you think there had to be a better way?

**Passive looking.** Looks for what kept the need quiet and what brought it back.
- Between that first thought and when you started looking seriously, what happened?
- Did you mention it to anyone?
- Did anything happen that made you think about it again?

**Active looking.** Looks for pull and the moment the need became urgent.
- When did you start comparing options?
- What moved you from thinking about it to doing something?
- What did you do first: search, ask a colleague, read reviews?
- Which options made your shortlist?

**Deciding.** Looks for the anxiety and habit that nearly stopped the switch.
- Walk me through the conversation where you decided.
- Who was involved?
- What was the single biggest factor?
- What almost made you choose differently?
- What worried you?

**First use.** Looks for anxiety after the decision and habits that stayed.
- Walk me through the first time you used it.
- What surprised you, good or bad?
- Did you go back to the old way for anything? What?

## Afterwards

Write a one-page summary within a day: the timeline, the struggling moment in the person's words, the four forces with quotations, hire and fire criteria, and anything that contradicts the team's assumptions. Keep exact quotations exact. Remove names and identifying details before the summary goes into workshop pre-work.
