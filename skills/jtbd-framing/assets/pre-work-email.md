# Workshop pre-work email

Send five to seven days before the workshop. Apply the sender's confirmed voice profile when one exists.

Subject: Pre-work for the {{DATE}} jobs-to-be-done workshop (about 30 minutes)

Hi {{TEAM}},

On {{DATE}} at {{TIME_AND_TIMEZONE}} ({{LOCATION_OR_LINK}}) we'll spend {{LENGTH}} working out what {{SEGMENT}} customers are trying to get done. The result feeds {{DECISION_AND_OWNER}}. We'll leave with a job hierarchy, scored desired outcomes, a forces map for the top job and opportunity statements with owners.

Please spend about 30 minutes before then:

1. Read the attached switch-interview summaries ({{COUNT}}, about 20 minutes). As you read, mark the first moment the person knew they needed something different, what pushed them away from the old way, what pulled them to the new one, what worried them, and what kept them where they were.
2. Write short answers to three questions (about 10 minutes):
   - In one sentence and without feature names, what is this customer trying to get done?
   - Pick three things customers ask us for. What outcome is behind each?
   - What in the summaries contradicts something we believe?

During the session we won't design solutions. Anything we can't trace to a summary or transcript goes on the list of hypotheses to test, and we'll steer from demographic labels to the situation the customer is in. Timeboxes are firm.

Bring your notes and a laptop{{OR_STICKY_NOTES_IF_IN_PERSON}}.

{{FACILITATOR_NAME}}

## Facilitator checklist before sending

- [ ] Summaries cover the evidence minimum for the format: two for two hours, four for a half day, six or more for a full day.
- [ ] Each summary fits on one page.
- [ ] Names, companies and other identifying details are removed.
- [ ] Calendar holds are sent.
- [ ] The board or printed canvases are ready.
- [ ] The live interview participant is confirmed for a full day.
