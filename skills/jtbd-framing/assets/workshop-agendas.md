# JTBD workshop agendas

Times are elapsed from the start. Each drafting block opens with five minutes of silent individual writing.

## Two hours: check a job hypothesis

| Start | Block | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Purpose, the decision this informs, and the job vocabulary (10 min) | Shared terms |
| 0:10 | Read two switch-interview summaries aloud (15 min) | Evidence in the room |
| 0:25 | Draft job statements, individually then together (20 min) | Five to ten candidate jobs, each citing a summary |
| 0:45 | Vote on the top three jobs (10 min) | Top three |
| 0:55 | Break each into three to five desired outcomes (30 min) | Outcome list |
| 1:25 | Forces map for the first job (20 min) | Push, pull, anxiety and habit with evidence numbers |
| 1:45 | Assign owners; list open hypotheses with the next research step (15 min) | Owner per outcome; questions to test |

## Half day: build the hierarchy before roadmap planning

| Start | Block | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Purpose and the decision; job vocabulary (20 min) | Shared terms |
| 0:20 | Review four to six switch-interview summaries (30 min) | Evidence in the room |
| 0:50 | Draft job statements individually (20 min) | Individual lists |
| 1:10 | Cluster into a main job and sub-jobs (40 min) | Job hierarchy |
| 1:50 | Break (10 min) | |
| 2:00 | Break the top three jobs into outcomes (45 min) | Outcomes per job |
| 2:45 | Score outcomes, labelled internal unless customer data exists (30 min) | Provisional ODI scores |
| 3:15 | Forces map for the top job (30 min) | Completed canvas |
| 3:45 | Opportunity statements, each with an owner and a next step (15 min) | Five to ten statements |

## Full day: new product, new segment or strategy reset

| Start | Block | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Purpose, decision, vocabulary and working agreements (30 min) | Ground rules |
| 0:30 | Live switch interview with a customer, recorded with consent (60 min) | First-hand evidence |
| 1:30 | Debrief the interview: what jobs and pains it showed, then which forces acted (30 min) | First snippets |
| 2:00 | Break (15 min) | |
| 2:15 | Six to ten more transcripts; build the snippet wall (60 min) | Coded snippets with evidence numbers |
| 3:15 | Lunch (45 min) | |
| 4:00 | Cluster snippets into a job hierarchy (60 min) | Main job and five to eight sub-jobs |
| 5:00 | Write desired outcomes (45 min) | 25 to 40 outcomes |
| 5:45 | Break (15 min) | |
| 6:00 | Score outcomes (45 min) | Provisional ODI scores |
| 6:45 | Forces maps for the top three jobs (45 min) | Three canvases |
| 7:30 | Opportunity statements and owners (30 min) | 10 to 15 statements |

Timebox the snippet wall. If the day runs late, protect the forces maps and the owner assignments.
