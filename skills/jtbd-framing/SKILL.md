# Jtbd framing

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For rendered artifacts, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md).

Start with a real struggling moment or decision from research. Identify the situation, desired progress, current alternative, constraints, and outcome. A feature request alone does not establish the underlying job.

Separate functional, emotional, and social aspects only where the evidence supports them. Identify what made a solution attractive, what made change difficult, and what would cause someone to stop using it. Preserve contradictory situations instead of forcing one universal persona.

Write a job statement that can change a product or positioning decision. Link it to evidence and an observable outcome. Keep proposals separate from what a customer actually said. Avoid inventing willingness to pay or emotional motives.

Test whether the framing distinguishes alternatives, improves discovery questions, or changes prioritization. If it merely restates an existing feature list, simplify it. Return the useful job framing and its decision implications, with remaining research questions.
