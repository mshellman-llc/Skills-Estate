# Delivery suite

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Read the executed scope and current delivery state. Identify the active phase, completed gates, owner of the next decision, and evidence required to advance. If the engagement has no state record, prepare one from evidence without inventing completed approvals.

Route handoff to s2d-handoff, requirements to requirements-capture, design to solution-brief or architecture-doc, ongoing reporting to delivery-status, and cutover/transition to cutover-closeout. Use the project's own kickoff, implementation, and UAT procedures for phases without a specialist skill.

Check whether requested work depends on an incomplete gate. Proceed with independent preparation, but do not mark a stage accepted, tested, or live merely because its deliverable exists. Record the decision holder and evidence needed to unblock dependent work.

After authorized work, verify the artifact and state change in their actual destinations. Report the stage, next gate, owner, and exact next action. Do not author an entirely new delivery methodology when the engagement already has a governing one.
