# Slide layout line budgets, Aleysian brand template

These budgets were originally measured from the retired `Aleysian-Brand-Template.pptx`. That template no longer ships; the budgets outlived it because they describe how much text fits, which the builder's own boxes reproduce. Verify by rendering when a slide changes shape. They are writing constraints, not design guidance. Every line budget assumes standard body font size (approximately 14–16pt) and standard text-frame margins. If a slide uses a non-standard font size, the budget changes, verify by rendering.

**Rule:** when text runs to the edge of the budget, cut words. Preserve the complete sentence; cut modifiers and prepositions first.

---

## Cover slide

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Document type label | 1 line, ~6 words max |
| Client + subtitle line | 1 line; use a centered dot (·) as separator, not an em dash |
| Date | 1 line |

Writing rule: the cover carries the client name and the document type only. No scope language, no teaser, no tagline.

---

## Agenda slide

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Section label per row | 1 line, ~5–7 words |
| Number of agenda items | 5–7 typical; 8 is the hard max before the layout collapses |

Writing rule: agenda labels are complete noun phrases, not sentences. They name what the slide covers, not what the audience will learn. "What pewag can't see today", not "We will discuss the current visibility gap."

No em dashes in agenda labels. Rephrase.

---

## Vertical-step layout (numbered steps / process flow)

This is the Aleysian template's single most constrained layout.

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Step header (the bold line above the support box) | 1 line, ~6 words max |
| Support box (the body text below the header) | **~2 lines** at standard font; 3 lines will collide with the next step header |
| Number of steps visible per slide | 4–5; 6 is possible only if support boxes are short |

Writing rule: the support box holds one complete sentence, sometimes two short ones if the second is under 8 words. Never three lines. If the content requires three lines, split into two steps or move to a bullet layout.

## Three-column card layout (version/options comparison)

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Card title | 1 line, ~4 words |
| Card label/pricing | 1 line |
| Card body | ~4–5 lines of short sentences OR ~3 longer sentences |

Writing rule: each card stands alone, a reader covering the other two cards should understand what this one says. No "Everything in Version 1, plus..." constructions. Write what the card contains, not what it inherits.

For the options/versions slide: Version 1 card gets "firm fixed-fee" or equivalent label. V2+ cards get "directional estimate." Never "TBD" or a blank.

---

## Full-body bullet layout (standard content slide)

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Slide title | 1 line, ~8 words |
| Subtitle / deck label | 1 line (optional) |
| Bullets | ~5–7 bullets at 1–2 lines each; 8+ bullets compress and become unreadable |

Writing rule: bullets are complete sentences. No hanging noun phrases ("Better visibility" alone as a bullet, write "Salesforce shows wear status for every chain set in the field."). One bullet, one idea.

Do not mix bullet lengths wildly. Two-line bullets and one-line bullets on the same slide are fine; a two-line bullet next to a five-line bullet signals the slide has a structural problem (split it or reframe the long one).

---

## Two-column layout (in scope / out of scope; now vs. later)

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Column header | 1 line, ~4 words |
| Items per column | ~5–8 at one line each |

Writing rule: items in each column are parallel, same grammatical form. If left column uses gerunds ("Migrating historical records"), right column uses them too ("Re-architecting the data model"). Do not mix gerunds and noun phrases.

Items are not full sentences here, short noun or gerund phrases are correct for this layout because the column header carries the predicate.

---

## Timeline / Gantt layout

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Phase label | 1 line, ~4 words |
| Phase duration | below the label; "Week 1–3" format |
| Key milestone | 1 line per row, ~6 words |

Writing rule: phase names are nouns, not verbs ("Foundation setup" not "Set up the foundation"). Duration must match the SOW. Milestones name the deliverable, not the activity ("Data migration complete" not "Migrate data").

---

## "What we need from you" layout (client responsibilities)

| Element | Budget |
|---------|--------|
| Item label | 1 line, ~5 words |
| Item support line | 1–2 lines |
| Number of items | 4–6 typical |

Writing rule: items are client asks, not internal requirements. Write from the client's perspective ("Designate a Salesforce admin with object-level access" not "Client must provide Salesforce admin access"). The distinction matters: one sounds like a partnership; the other sounds like a contract obligation.

---

## Thank-you / closing slide

No copy budget, the slide is visual only. If any copy appears (website, contact), one line. No summary, no restatement of scope, no "we look forward to partnering with you."
