# Optional Knowledge Hub adapter

Use a hub only when the product has an authorized destination and the requested action permits capture or update. Read the product's integration guidance and the destination's current contract. Confirm the account and space against the access boundary. Check source authority and the supported write mechanism. A configured connector or named local path cannot establish private access or write authorization.

The product home remains the entry point unless its existing convention explicitly makes the hub authoritative. The hub can hold a retrieval projection of reviewed product records and their evidence; it must not become an accidental second decision register.

## Capture sequence

1. Read the destination contract and relevant records completely at exact revisions. If unavailable, keep the local work and record the adapter gap.
2. Select only reviewed content allowed inside that sharing boundary. Exclude secrets and unreviewed bulk extracts. Private evidence needs authorization for that destination. Retain source IDs and hashes/revisions. Record the review boundary and unresolved conflicts.
3. Prepare the update against the exact expected record and contract revisions. Use the supported proposal or authorized bridge mechanism; do not overwrite synchronized Markdown or the live database directly.
4. Submit only within existing authorization. An uncertain response requires reading the destination before retrying. On a revision conflict, fetch the current version and reconcile; do not overwrite it with a blind retry. Stop when the conflict needs a material owner decision or access is unavailable.
5. Read back the accepted body at its returned revision; verify its source binding. Test retrieval through the intended product route. Preserve the submission receipt. Record acceptance separately from retrieval verification, with a receipt for each.

## Maintenance boundary

Bind each projection to the current product records. When a source or decision changes, record the impact and refresh only affected authorized projections. Keep staleness and incomplete-coverage warnings visible where retrieval depends on them. A saved warning does not prove runtime enforcement.

Capture does not authorize a new account connection, private cloud sharing, automatic ingestion, cross-product learning, or scheduled upkeep. Preserve any existing authorized adapter; a product without one still uses the full local pathway.
