# Sticker art direction

Use for sticker concepts, typography refinement, collection direction, and visual critique. For imposing existing PNGs onto cutting sheets, use the project's production workflow. If a sheet-layout skill is unavailable, inspect the existing generator and machine profile; do not redesign the approved images or guess cut settings. This skill does not select a political stance, invent a brand identity, or authorize publication.

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md) and [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md). Resolve the current project brief and decisions from its `AGENTS.md` or index. Keep project identities, private phrases, fonts, and licensed artwork in the project. If no brief exists, infer a small working brief from the request and label the assumptions; do not require an intake interview. For unbranded artwork, propose an artifact-scoped type and color treatment from the request; identify it as a proposal. The neutral reporting style is not a requirement to use system fonts in expressive artwork.

## Decide the visual idea

Preserve supplied copy and meaningful informality. Treat a substantive rewrite or punctuation change as a visible alternative. Identify the phrase's function and intended reading order. Direct statements need not become jokes.

Use documented references for a specific mechanism, such as a line break, density, rhythm, or relationship between text and object. Cite the actual artifact or designer source. Study more than one source before delivering a developed new visual direction. An early brief may name reference questions to investigate without pretending that research has already occurred; a narrow correction may need no new research. Do not reproduce a recognizable composition, lettering asset, or trade dress from a reference.

When alternatives would help, make them structurally distinct: change reading order, alignment, typographic voice, density, or silhouette for a reason. Font and color swaps on one layout are auditions, not different concepts. Match the number of alternatives to the decision; a narrow correction may need only one.

## Set and refine the type

Read [typography and critique](references/typography.md). Audition the actual words in real, available font files. Assess individual letterforms, width, weight, contrast, and behavior at the intended output size. Do not infer font rights from installation or embedding flags.

Preserve font shaping and kerning as the starting point. Avoid independently scaling every line to a common width, synthetic stretching, and automatic optical kerning across scripts or monospace text. Apply changes only to demonstrated problems. Let the outer shape and composition develop together.

Use a renderer that can preserve the selected fonts, shaping, editable copy, and intended geometry. Programmatic typesetting is acceptable when its output is visually reviewed. An image generator may support illustration or exploratory imagery when requested; exact final text needs a reliable lettering workflow.

## Critique before expanding

Inspect the rendered proof closely and at the proposed physical size or a clearly labeled scale preview. Check reading order, meaning, tone, optical spacing, composition, and reproduction separately. Numerical fit checks do not establish visual quality. Critique with concrete observations and recommend a route; do not automatically praise every option.

Develop a representative approved direction before extending it across a collection. Approval of one proof does not freeze every design choice into a brand rule. Record decisions with the artifact, version, and scope.

## Deliver and hand off

Read [production and sources](references/production.md) when exporting or preparing print work. Deliver inspectable proofs, editable wording and layout source, exact font records, and an outlined or otherwise stable viewing export when appropriate. Do not bundle commercial font files without redistribution rights.

Keep source, screen proof, print-tested artwork, and approved production files distinct. Use the current project folder and index. Explain the meaningful design choice and exact next action; leave internal checks off the artwork. Publication, sales claims, font purchases, and cloud uploads follow the user's specific authorization.
