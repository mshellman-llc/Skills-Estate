# Delivery kickoff

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For rendered artifacts, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md).

Read the signed SOW, accepted handoff, current engagement record, delivery model, and assigned owners. Preserve the executed scope and commercial terms. Record missing acceptance or ownership rather than inventing it.

Prepare a charter covering outcomes, scope, boundaries, deliverables, responsibilities, cadence, decision rights, dependencies, risks, and acceptance. Seed risk, assumption, issue, dependency, and decision logs with stable IDs and meaningful owners and dates where known.

Build internal and client kickoff materials for their distinct audiences. Create the initial plan or Sprint 0 backlog with prerequisites, access dependencies, environments, test preparation, and early validation. Separate planned setup from completed setup.

Track access and sandbox provisioning without claiming it happened. Perform an operational setup only when that action is authorized and the target is verified. Save the governance baseline and report the next gate and its owner. A draft charter is not a signature or kickoff acceptance.
