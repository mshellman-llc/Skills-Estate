# Call prep

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Choose a pursuit brief or one upcoming call. Read the account and opportunity record, existing brief, relevant correspondence, and dated research before asking the user to repeat them.

For the pursuit brief, record the client's situation, desired outcomes, qualification evidence, known stakeholders, risks, decisions needed, and next actions. Separate confirmed facts, assumptions with their basis, and unresolved questions. Recommend the roles still needed without inventing people. Each recommended call has a purpose, desired outcome, and prerequisites.

For an upcoming call, state the objective and decisions, attendees and roles, known context, timed agenda, ranked questions, sensitivities, and follow-up plan. Link it to the pursuit brief. Reuse the discovery ledger's open questions; add only questions specific to this meeting.

Verify company and attendee facts in current sources when the task depends on them. Distinguish the client's words from sales hypotheses. Do not infer budget, buying authority, attendance, or commitment. Save updates to the agreed record and identify the evidence that should return after the call. Preparing the agenda does not schedule or send it.
