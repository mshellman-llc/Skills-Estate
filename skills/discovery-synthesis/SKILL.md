# Discovery synthesis

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Read the supplied meeting material and identify session date, purpose, participants, and scope. Preserve speaker uncertainty when labels are missing. A summary is secondary evidence; do not promote it to a verbatim transcript.

Extract jobs to be done as situation, desired progress, and outcome. Describe pain points with their observed frequency or severity and supporting evidence. Separate reported pain from your interpretation of its cause. Connect opportunity areas to those findings without implying that a proposed solution was requested.

Retain useful exact quotes with speaker and timestamp or source location. Never improve a quote's grammar or invent a representative quote. Group related findings, retain meaningful dissent, and distinguish one person's observation from a shared pattern.

Deliver a concise session summary, the research findings, selected verbatims, and next steps with owners where known. Use the user's requested format. If the work needs to update a pursuit's ranked questions, hand the findings to discovery-brief. A locally saved synthesis is not uploaded to a knowledge product until that destination confirms it.
