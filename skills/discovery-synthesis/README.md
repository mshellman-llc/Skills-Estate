# Discovery Synthesis

> Transform raw discovery call content, notes, transcripts, Granola/Fathom recordings, or uploaded files, into structured research outputs: JTBD statements, pain points with severity signals, opportunity areas, key verbatims, speaker-attributed insights, a TL;DR, and next steps.

**What it does.** Synthesizes discovery calls, interview notes and transcripts into sourced findings: job statements, pain points with severity, exact verbatims, speaker-attributed insights, a TL;DR, open questions and next steps. Every finding carries its source location and speaker role, and quotations keep the speaker's exact words.

**Pursuit findings.** Select the embedded maintain pursuit findings mode for the deal discovery record, ranked ledger and open questions. It retains the complete discovery-brief workflow and its evidence checks.
