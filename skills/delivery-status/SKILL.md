# Delivery status

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Pin the engagement, reporting start and end dates, previous report, signed scope, current plan, risk log, and decision log. Read only evidence relevant to this period plus unresolved items carried forward. A first report has no prior-period delta unless a baseline exists.

Update stable IDs for risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies, and decisions. Keep a dependency separate from a decision. Include owner, due date when known, state, and supporting evidence. A participant's request does not become a decision without an actual decision holder.

Compare new asks with the scope baseline. Describe potential change, affected obligation, evidence, impact needing analysis, and owner of the disposition. Do not price or approve the change. Carry unresolved candidates forward without duplicating their IDs.

Write status in terms of actual progress, milestones, supported financial or effort data, blockers, decisions, and next actions. Compute deltas and reconcile any burn figures to their source. Verify every completed claim against delivered work or acceptance evidence. Save the report and logs when authorized; preparing an update does not send it.
