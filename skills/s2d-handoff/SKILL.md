# S2d handoff

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Verify whether the deal is signed. A prepared SOW or sales stage does not establish execution. Use the executed agreement and amendments first, then approved quote and scope, confirmed decisions, and supporting discovery evidence. Keep discrepancies visible.

Assemble the business outcomes, promised deliverables, scope boundaries, acceptance mechanics, commercial model, timeline, staffing assumptions, stakeholder roles, dependencies, risks, technical decisions, and open actions. Include the source location for obligations and decisions that delivery must inherit.

Explain the delivery implications of each material assumption and unresolved dependency. Record owner and closure condition for gaps. Do not convert a sales suggestion into an obligation or a handoff meeting into acceptance.

Prepare the transfer agenda and package. Record the receiving owner's actual acceptance, date, and any conditions only when supported. Seed the delivery state record with the accepted baseline and next gate when authorized. Keep preparation, transfer, acceptance, kickoff, and delivery completion as separate states.
