# Range discipline

The ROM's headline is a range, and the range is a claim. These rules keep it defensible.

## The anchor

The quote total is the anchor. The range brackets it: low bound at or below the quote total,
high bound at or above it. A range that does not contain its own quote total is an arithmetic
failure and the gate rejects it. The skill never moves the anchor; that is the SSL's and deal
desk's number.

## Band width has a stated basis

The width is never decoration. It is driven by, and its basis is printed beside it:

1. **What the source states.** If the quote, playbook definition, or deal desk has stated a
   band (for example plus or minus 10 percent on a fixed-fee engagement), use it and cite it.
   This is the normal case.
2. **If no band is stated**, propose one and label it "proposed, pending SSL confirmation".
   The proposal widens with the confidence composition: a BOE dominated by Confirmed rows
   supports a tight band; every load-bearing Unknown widens it. A deal touching a
   maturity-gap capability (per the firm's confidence tiers: CPQ and Billing amendments, ERP
   integrations, RCA migrations, DRO, UBB, CLM) widens the range rather than thinning the
   number. A proposed band is a routing to a human, not a decision.

Never narrow a band to make the number more attractive. It is better to raise the price or
widen the range than to present scope Estate cannot deliver.

## Pricing model handling

The model comes from the quote. Never both, in any section, on any view.

**Fixed fee.** Present the fixed price and its milestone basis, with the band around it.
Phase-by-role and work-package detail render if the quote carries them.

**Time and materials.** Present the dollar envelope plus the upfront percentage and terms. Do
not draw a plus-or-minus band; the envelope is the range. Hours are planning guidelines, not
fixed allocations, and reaching the envelope is itself a Change Order trigger. Do not present
a phase-by-role cross-tab or per-work-package hours unless the quote carries them; flag their
absence as inherent to the model rather than filling it.

## Range reopeners

Name the specific conditions under which the range no longer holds. Each reopener is read
from this deal's scope items and BOE rows, not from a generic list. Two kinds:

- **Soft reopeners**: a BOE volumetric exceeded (the assumed 4 integrations become 6). The
  BOE row states the assumed value; the reopener states what exceeding it means.
- **Hard reopeners**: binary scope additions that are Change Orders regardless of size (a new
  business unit, a platform not in scope, a data source not named).

## Honesty in presentation

- State the as-of date of the quote snapshot next to the range. A range outlives its inputs
  quickly.
- The buyer view shows the range and its basis. The confidence composition and the printed
  reconciliation belong on the deal-desk view.
- If footprint dimensions are unconfirmed, the range is wide. Say so on the page. Do not
  present an Unknown-heavy ROM with the same framing as a Confirmed-heavy one.
