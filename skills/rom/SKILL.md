# Rom

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Resolve the current quote or authorized estimate model, commercial model, audience, and source revision. Use the source's phases and rates. If no authoritative rates or effort basis exist, produce the scope assumptions and missing inputs instead of fabricating an investment figure.

Separate stated inputs, computed figures, and estimates. Explain the range through known uncertainty: volume, integration count, migration quality, customization, security, testing, adoption, and delivery constraints as relevant. Wider uncertainty needs a wider or unpriced outcome; do not manufacture confidence percentages.

Reconcile hours by work package, role, and phase. Compute hours times rates and explain any difference from a fixed quoted fee using the quote's actual commercial rules. Check that dates and staffing capacity support the timeline. Tie the strongest cost drivers to observable dimensions.

Deliver the range, scope basis, dependencies, exclusions, confidence limits, and specific events that reopen the estimate. Distinguish fixed-fee commitments from time-and-materials assumptions. A ROM is a planning estimate until the pricing source and authorized owner establish a firm offer.
