# Business case

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For rendered artifacts, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md).

Identify the approver, decision, current situation, proposed intervention, timing, and source of financial figures. Use evidence-core to separate measured facts, reported expectations, estimates, and assumptions.

Compare the proposal with doing nothing and credible alternatives. Include benefits, implementation and operating costs, opportunity costs, dependencies, risks, and reasons not to invest. Do not assume that a vendor's projected benefit is realized value.

Show calculations for savings, capacity, revenue, payback, or cost of delay. Use sensitivity scenarios only with explicit inputs; avoid false precision and double-counted benefits. Identify who can validate the assumptions and who owns realization.

Bound the ask by amount, scope, stage, conditions, decision owner, and next review. Render for the approver's actual decision, preserving material uncertainties. A business case supports an investment decision; it does not record approval until the approver actually gives it.
