# Quality gate

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Identify the audience, purpose, authoritative inputs, exact artifact revision, and requested destination. Read the finished content, including relevant notes and metadata. A checker result is evidence about its checks, not a substitute for reading the artifact.

Review factual support and scope, representation of the author's work, writing clarity, and layout separately. Compute totals and verify dates, cross-references, links, and stated decisions. Apply the shared brand policy for rendered artifacts and the author's confirmed writing profile where relevant.

Use available deterministic checks for structural defects and inspect rendered output when presentation matters. Record the checks actually performed and their result. Never manufacture a receipt, reviewer, approval, visual inspection, or passing test.

Fix authorized defects and rerun affected checks. For an unresolved issue, state its effect and the exact next action. Keep local preparation, saved destination, publication, acceptance, and runtime behavior distinct. Do not call a source or artifact fully reviewed if any required part was clipped or inaccessible.
