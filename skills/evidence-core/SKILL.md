# Evidence core

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Resolve the subject authority and current source versions before extracting claims. Record source ID, title, location, date, revision or hash, owner where known, and access limitations. A partially read source cannot support a claim of full review.

Represent each claim separately with its evidence location, subject, time range, scope, and status: confirmed, reported, estimated, proposed, disputed, or unknown. Keep estimates' inputs and calculations. Do not turn repeated mentions of one source into independent corroboration.

Preserve contradictory records and describe the unresolved conflict. Refresh volatile facts at the live destination. Missing evidence does not prove an event never occurred. An archive is dated evidence and does not silently replace the current authority.

Before handing evidence to an artifact skill, check that it supports the requested decision, including important omissions and qualifications. Keep private evidence out of public deliverables. Update synchronized stores through their revision-safe interface; do not bypass governance by editing a database or mirrored file directly.
