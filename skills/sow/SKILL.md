# Sow

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For a rendered or branded artifact, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md) before choosing visual values. These references are generated from the canonical policies when the estate builds.

Read the current quote, scope baseline, contracting entities, chosen legal template, and applicable agreement. Resolve the source revision and requested variant: fixed deliverables, adaptive backlog/capacity, or assessment and readout.

Build a source-to-document mapping for scope, deliverables, responsibilities, acceptance, timeline, pricing, payment milestones, assumptions, exclusions, and change control. Use legal clauses and payment details supplied for this engagement. This skill contains no reusable payment account or preapproved legal terms.

If approved terms or entity details are missing, prepare a labeled draft with unresolved fields visible to the reviewer. Do not replace them with terms from another engagement or treat a historical clause as approved. Preserve contractual meaning while editing prose.

Use the available document tool for the requested editable file. Reconcile totals and milestones, check every cross-reference, resolve placeholders against the mapping, and compare required clauses with the supplied template. Run sow-quality for scope and structure review. Rendering a document does not approve its legal terms, send it to the client, or execute it.
