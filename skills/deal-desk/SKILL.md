# Deal desk

Read [shared workflow](references/estate/workflow.md). For rendered artifacts, read [brand policy](references/estate/brand.md).

Read the authoritative quote revision, pricing model, cost basis, internal approval policy, and relevant draft artifacts. Keep internal rates, costs, margins, and approval commentary out of client-facing files.

Compute revenue, delivery cost, gross margin, discounts, and other requested measures from actual inputs. Show formulas and reconcile displayed figures across quote, scope, staffing, timeline, and commercial documents. Do not use an inherited margin floor or approval ladder; read the current business policy.

Identify exceptions, risks, missing inputs, contractual deviations, capacity assumptions, and the decision needed. Route each exception to the actual approval owner. If a threshold or cost basis is unavailable, report the missing input and do not invent a pass.

Deliver the review packet and exact figure differences. A reviewer-ready packet is not commercial approval. Changing a quote or issuing a client proposal requires the user's authorization for that action.
