Fill one canvas per job. Put an evidence number beside every entry that came from an interview or record; entries without one are hypotheses.

| Toward switching | Against switching |
|---|---|
| **Push of the situation.** What is bad about the current way? {{PUSH_ENTRIES}} | **Anxiety about the new way.** What worries the person about switching? {{ANXIETY_ENTRIES}} |
| **Pull of the new way.** What is attractive about the alternative? {{PULL_ENTRIES}} | **Habit of the present.** What keeps the person on the current way? {{HABIT_ENTRIES}} |

A switch happens when push and pull together outweigh anxiety and habit.

## What the canvas suggests

Teams tend to invest in pull. Check whether the canvas shows anxiety and habit as the larger barriers.

| Force | Typical moves |
|---|---|
| Reduce anxiety | Free trial, refund guarantee, migration help, proof from comparable customers, security certifications |
| Reduce habit | Import tools, side-by-side comparison, a period of using both |
| Sharpen push | Make the cost of the current way visible in onboarding and messaging |
| Refine pull | Name specific advantages against named alternatives, not general benefits |

Each move is a proposal for the owning team, not a finding.
