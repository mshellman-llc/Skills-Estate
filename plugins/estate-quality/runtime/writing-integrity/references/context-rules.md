# Requirements for particular kinds of writing

Read the section for the document you are editing. Preserve these facts and conditions while applying the author's confirmed voice.

## Corrections across formats

An end reader is not a participant in the drafting conversation. After correcting an AI misunderstanding, keep the subject and reader’s task at the center of the finished work. Do not make a heading, introduction, diagram branch, UI label or interaction explain the former mistake. Test the result without chat history. Explanations of the correction belong in the response to the requester when needed; errata and change notices are exceptions when the audience needs the correction itself.

## Leadership and career

Preserve career progression, senior leadership and ownership. Do not recast a leader as a generic individual contributor or assume the work was primarily billable delivery. Include technical go-to-market, pre-sales, program leadership and applied AI when the source supports them.

Describe people leadership without inventing team size, reporting lines or hiring authority. Make the hiring case clear through the person's work. Remove generic praise for the employer, keyword stuffing, defensive disclaimers and inflated claims. Distinguish base salary from total compensation.

## Commercial and delivery

In a full proposal or option comparison, keep every supplied price, hour estimate, role, assumption, exclusion, date and qualification unless an authorized source changes it. A requested summary may select details, but must retain the conditions and approval state needed to interpret its claims and make the requested decision. Distinguish included work from added options; do not broaden scope when shortening it.

Identify estimates, client reports, proposals, approvals and open questions accurately. “Proposed” cannot become “committed”; “built locally” cannot become “live.” Keep the full scope behind an executive summary, including conditions and unresolved evidence the reader needs.

## Presentations and visual documents

For a visual-only change, preserve the approved text, figures, tables, order and qualifications. Keep facilitator notes out of audience copy unless requested. Keep each source and qualification beside the claim it supports.

Shortening text for a slide must preserve approval state, certainty, ownership and scope.

## Reflective or personal writing

Preserve uncertainty, context and self-correction when the writer is still working out a thought. Use scenes and examples from the source; do not invent a personal story.

Do not force a binary position, polished thesis or motivational ending. Keep unfinished or rough wording when it carries the writer's personality or feeling.

## Sensitive internal or personal messages

Begin with the decision, boundary or requested action. Include only the sensitive details the recipient needs. For transition help, exceptions or support, specify who is responsible, what is included and when it ends.

Check records for claims such as “complete,” “approved” or “remaining.” State when they are unverified if the reader needs that status. Make the next action specific without creating an obligation the writer did not authorize.

## Organizational or regulated writing

Preserve legal, contractual, regulatory and approved organizational wording. Keep defined terms and mandated disclosures verbatim.

Apply the confirmed brand's vocabulary and presentation rules after protecting facts and legal meaning. When writing for another person, use that person's supplied voice.
