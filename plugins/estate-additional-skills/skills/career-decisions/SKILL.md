# Career progression and offers

Runs beside `career-application`. Four jobs: a progression check on one role, a portfolio view across the tracker, negotiation prep once a number is in play, and an offer comparison. Megan decides; this skill produces facts and drafts.

Before starting, load `00_Master/Career_Application_Operating_Context.md` (target-role screen, October 7 career direction), the role's `Role_Work_Record.md`, `03_Tracker/applications.md`, `05_Reference/Career_Strategy/Career_Paths.md`, and the live posting or written offer. The career-application assess screen still runs; this adds the level view the operating context asks for.

## Gates

Show each result; a failed gate is never averaged into a score.

- Base at or above $200K, target $250K or more, base only. No posted base → open question, not a pass.
- Billability: nonbillable preferred; a billable component is negotiable only when expected billability is strictly below 25%, so 25% fails. Unknown → unresolved question to ask; never assume zero from a leadership title, presales work or an employment contract. Owning a company's own internal platforms is not billable delivery.
- Not a Solution Architect role or an equivalent individual-contributor solution-design or delivery-resource role, whatever the title. Architecture experience is leadership evidence only.
- Scope: compare authority against her Senior Director and practice-lead history, not titles. Unknown scope → unresolved.
- Alignment: record any flag Megan or the research raised (industry, customers, politics) as a line under the record's Current decision, with her stated reason when she chooses to pursue. Recorded, never scored.

## Progression matrix

The destination is a path to VP and eventually an executive leadership team seat. For each of the operating context's eight criteria (strategy ownership, decision authority, budget or resource accountability, people or organizational leadership, enterprise outcomes, executive exposure, reporting line, next leadership level) record one of three states: verified (an employer statement in the posting, offer letter or interview), inferred (plausible from role shape, labeled), unknown. A Director title, a VP reporting line or a recruiter's promise is inferred at most; a promotion is verified only in writing.

Score verified 2, inferred 1, unknown 0 per criterion, weighted. Weights are Megan's; ask once, record them in the operating context's target-role screen when she gives them, and use equal weights until then. Report the three column counts beside the score so a high score built on inference is visible. Keep the matrix in the record under Screen and update it after every interview round.

Level red flags in a posting: "hands-on", "player-coach", "individual contributor", "utilization" or "billable" targets, "architect" in the title or first responsibility, no direct reports, a reporting line below VP, delivery hidden in "other duties", two roles written into one posting. Compensation red flags: "competitive salary", "DOE", equity-heavy with no base. A repost needs a reason found.

Discovery questions start from "Questions for the employer" in `04_Interview/Interview_Preparation.md` and add only these progression questions — who held this role last and where did they go; what the role decides on budget and hiring without escalation; who sits on the ELT and how this role reaches them; expected billable share in hours; who has been promoted from this level in the last two years.

## Portfolio view

On request, run the gates and the matrix across every nonterminal row in the tracker and add one line per role on lane coherence against the primary identity in `Career_Paths.md` (Solution Engineering and Technical GTM leadership; applied AI differentiator; revenue technology foundation). Note where an in-house IT seat, a RevOps lens or a consulting title pulls away from that identity. When a role closes, record why in its work record so the next search learns from it.

## Negotiation

- Research with WebSearch at each offer stage: posted bands for comparable Director and Senior Director roles in GTM systems, revenue technology and solutions engineering; the employer's own ranges; levels data where it exists. Cite source and date. Without search, use the employer's posted range with a dated note.
- Megan supplies the number. Draft nothing for a salary field or a recruiter's expectations question until she gives a figure. Default, which she can change: do not disclose past pay.
- Counter on base first. Secondary levers in the order that serves the destination: a title on the VP line, a written scope and decision-authority statement, a dated review tied to a level, ELT meeting access, signing bonus, equity, start date, remote and travel terms, budget or headcount for the function. A verbal promise not in the offer letter stays unknown.
- Deliver a counter email and a call script with answers to "this is our best offer", "the band is fixed" and "we need an answer by Friday", in Megan's voice. Drafts only; she sends or says it.

## Offer comparison

Ask for these inputs before building:

- base;
- target bonus and realistic attainment;
- equity with valuation assumption, vesting and cliff;
- signing bonus;
- benefits differences;
- relocation;
- travel and office days;
- expected billability;
- reporting line and title;
- anything verified in writing about promotion.

Show base apart from everything else, with a year-one and a four-year view. Lay out the gates per offer, the matrix with its three columns, the weighted score, the strongest case for each offer and the open questions on each. Do not pick. Two or more offers or scenarios go in a Codex Sheets artifact; a single offer goes in a Codex doc. Save once under `03_Tracker/` with a pointer from each role folder. Tracker status (`offer`, `closed`) changes only on Megan's word.

Update the role work record and tracker. End with changed, verified, unchanged and the exact next action.
