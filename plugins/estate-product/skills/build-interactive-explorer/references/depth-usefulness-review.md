# Review scope, depth and practical usefulness

Use this for a substantial field guide, solution explanation, project reference, or adaptation of supplied work. Keep it within the existing writing, visual and interaction reviews. This is a substantive judgment, not another reviewer gate, section quota or word-count target.

## Preserve the requested work before choosing a focal view

Identify the requested deliverable, readers and working questions. Inventory the relevant source substance and the capabilities demonstrated by supplied positive examples. Map each material item to a real output location, an explicit source gap, or a user-approved omission. A reader-friendly overview may lead the work; it does not replace the complete reference.

For a system or project field guide, check the source-supported coverage that the request needs: business process and role handoffs; system boundaries and integrations; record relationships and consequential fields; design and automation rules; permissions and access assumptions; sequencing, ownership and operational recovery; scope, dependencies and unresolved design choices; worked examples, alternatives and failure paths. This is a coverage lens, not a requirement to invent every category or use these as headings.

Do not silently change a full guide into a forecast model, executive summary, vendor directory or one mechanism. Preserve original assets, screenshots, meaningful interactions and examples when adapting supplied work; anonymize consistently when needed. If a source is unavailable, name the gap rather than write an invented substitute. Writing polish changes expression while retaining relevant technical depth and qualifications.

## Compare the actual content with the supplied examples

Read the relevant reference content and meaningful states. Compare every applicable reference method, not just the easiest favorable match. State the inspected reference and section, whether it was source-inspected or rendered, the useful capability it provides, and what equivalent value the new artifact provides. Do not copy private reference facts into another subject. A reference's visual direction is not blanket approval of its historical claims.

Use these lenses when demonstrated by the references:

- A technical field guide can expose named records and ownership, interfaces with exchanged values and cadence, operational sequences, consequential rules and recovery branches. A box naming a system does not supply this depth.
- A commercial flow can explain what creates value, how pricing or volume changes it, where loss occurs and which action follows. A decorative arrow or a paragraph-swapping vendor selector is not equivalent.
- A phased solution can connect a delivered capability to the changed business step, its dependencies, retained limitations and decisions. A stage list alone does not explain what can be done.
- A working brief can connect a concrete concern to its supported explanation, evidence and dependency ask. Use rehearsal only when it serves the current task; keep working-discussion rules and open choices visible together when appropriate.

Record a compact comparison for the review: reference capability; exact output location/state; depth and useful action demonstrated; omissions or superficial treatment; verdict and repair. A heading, a source link, matching colors or a component count is not evidence that the capability is present. Compare the initial view and important detail, not only screenshots of closed controls.

## Demonstrate practical value and find the gaps

Use a representative working question and an exception or change that matter to this subject. Trace the actual records, fields, inputs, rules, handoffs and consequences through the output. Can the reader explain why the system behaves that way, assess an assumption or tradeoff, diagnose a failure, or identify the decision needed next? Cite the artifact locations that make this possible and the missing detail that prevents it.

A useful numerical illustration can prove one mechanism. It does not establish that the system designs, access model, integrations and wider operational guide exist. Likewise, readable text and functioning controls do not establish substantive usefulness. Review the whole requested scope separately from the quality of a selected section.

## Give an honest verdict

Begin with sufficient, partial or insufficient depth and usefulness for the requested deliverable, with reasons grounded in the comparison. List the strongest useful explanation and the most important omissions. Treat missing material scope, generic summaries in place of mechanisms, and incomplete failure or assumption coverage as content findings requiring repair. Do not excuse them because the page looks polished or has no broken controls.

Keep this verdict separate from visual craft, functional checks, reviewer invocation and the user's acceptance. A user rejection remains a rejection even if all those checks ran. Disclose access limits and unapproved omissions before claiming completion. Fix findings at the source and retest affected cases; do not add broad estate checks or repeat independent reviews without a new change or unresolved concern.
