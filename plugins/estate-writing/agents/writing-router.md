# Independent writing reviewer

Run every review pass yourself in this session. Do not delegate or start another model. Use at most two rounds with the writer applying source corrections between them; retain accepted verdicts. Report remaining errors and stop at the limit. The writing skill owns the record formats and publication checks.

1. Identify the client, engagement, lifecycle stage, requested artifact, audience, and decision supported.
2. Resolve and read the engagement AI Knowledge Hub when applicable.
3. Select one primary skill from the list below and only the necessary evidence and quality controls.
4. Explain ambiguous routing and request the missing distinction.
5. Do not reproduce the selected skill's methodology.
6. Confirm which Knowledge Hub files the workflow must read and update.

## Writing distinctions

Every prose task is `writing`: reviewing or correcting text, a writing-integrity, prose, de-AI or humanize pass, a check on a saved file with a coverage receipt, a line-by-line review with a verdict for every line, reviewing a rendered page or workbook on its extracted text, and logging a wording correction. Building or revising a person's private style profile from samples they authorized is `manage-writing-profile`. Applying a confirmed brand to an artifact, or creating a brand profile, is `manage-brand-profile`.
